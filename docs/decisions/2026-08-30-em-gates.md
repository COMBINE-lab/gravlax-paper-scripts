# Frozen gates — EM multimapper recovery with cross-cell information sharing

**Date:** 2026-08-30 · **Status: FROZEN before implementation.** Funded by D33: 691,030
gene-informative classes (+6.25% of counted UMIs) are discarded today; 0.91M mixed classes are
counted with their multi-gene evidence ignored.

## Design (fixed before any number is seen)

Per (cell, class): the gene-support vector — for every gene concordant with any of the class's
rows, its unique-row and multi-row read weights. Single-gene classes count as today (they also
estimate the expression weights π). Ambiguous classes (≥2 distinct genes) receive fractional
assignments r_{u,g} ∝ π_g over their candidate sets; M-step re-estimates π from unique + assigned
mass; 10 iterations. Three sharing scopes, the experiment's subject:

- **cell**: π estimated within each cell only (STARsolo-EM-style locality).
- **pooled**: one global π across all cells (maximal sharing — the archive holds the whole
  sample's evidence at once, which is the capability being demonstrated).
- **blend**: r ∝ u_cell,g + α·f_global,g with α = 20 pre-registered (sensitivity over
  α ∈ {5, 20, 80} reported, not tuned-then-chosen).

Baseline: uniform over the candidate set. All modes share identical candidate sets and truth.

## EM-1 — masked-evidence recovery (labeled truth on real data)

Protocol: hold out 20% of MIXED classes (seeded, deterministic); strip their single-gene rows so
they become indistinguishable from the discarded multi-only pool; truth = the gene their unique
evidence supported (what Gene counting counts today). Metrics on held-out classes whose truth
gene remains in the masked candidate set (coverage reported separately): top-1 accuracy
(argmax r = truth) and expected accuracy (r on truth), mass-weighted.

| Verdict | Criterion |
|---|---|
| **PASS** | pooled or blend top-1 ≥ 75%, AND best sharing mode beats uniform by ≥15 points AND beats cell-only (any margin) |
| **MARGINAL** | best mode ≥ 60% and beats uniform by ≥10 |
| **STOP** | no mode beats uniform by ≥5 points — sharing adds nothing; drop the EM contribution |

## EM-2 — matrix impact (measurement, reported not thresholded)

With the winning mode: total recovered mass (expect ≈ +6%), its gene-level concentration (top
recipient genes/families — paralog families expected; anything else is suspicious), and the
fraction of recovered mass assigned with r > 0.8 (confident) vs diffuse.

## Deferred

EM-0 (byte-level replication of STARsolo `--soloMultiMappers EM`) is a separate fidelity gate,
deferred: it needs an oracle re-run and a port of STAR's EM; the sharing question above does not
depend on it.

## EM-0 (added 2026-08-30, frozen before the oracle run finished): STARsolo EM replication

Oracle: fresh STARsolo `--soloMultiMappers EM` (v49, matrix-only run, `runs/oracle/full/v49mm`).
Replay: `aie em --star` (per-cell EM, intersection candidate sets, STAR's init/zero/convergence
constants, unique side = the byte-exact Gene replay). Comparison on filtered cells,
UniqueAndMult-EM vs UniqueAndMult-EM: **PASS** mass-weighted rel. L1 ≤1%; **MARGINAL** ≤3%;
**STOP** >10%.
